Vietnam, 1965 to 1969 · Lesson 3 of 5
Where we are

Your essay already argues that the war began anti-colonial, became a Cold War fight, and that America propped up a government that was its own worst enemy. Now, in 1965, America stops propping up and takes over. It brings the most powerful military on earth. And it loses. Your job: work out why, and add that to the essay "to what extent were the Americans responsible for their own defeat?"

The essay so far

Your argument now has three moves. The war began anti-colonial and became a Cold War fight. America backed a government that drove its own people to the enemy. And when America took over the fighting, its overwhelming power turned on itself: the bombing, the burned villages, the poisoned land and the atrocities lost the war among the Vietnamese and, once it was on television every night, at home too.

Winning every battle and still losing is one of the hardest ideas in history, and you just argued it. It is also worth remembering the next time someone with all the power assumes that power alone will be enough.
